# Five whys: why do drafted reports still take adviser time?

Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed. Not affiliated with Saturn.

Starting point: Saturn publishes that suitability reports now take 20 minutes of review, and all AI documentation stays subject to mandatory adviser review ([ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) S6).

1. **Why does review still take time?** Because the adviser has to read every sentence and decide whether it is true for this client.
2. **Why read every sentence?** Because nothing on the page tells them which sentences have already been checked and which have not. A correct figure and an invented one look the same.
3. **Why do they look the same?** Because the draft is shown as prose, separated from the transcript and fact-find it came from. The link between sentence and source exists inside the drafting step but is not shown at review.
4. **Why does that matter beyond speed?** Because the adviser signs the report and carries the regulatory responsibility. Without the link, review is either slow (re-derive everything) or risky (skim and trust). Neither leaves evidence a compliance reviewer can audit.
5. **Why has review not been split already?** Because figures and judgement are mixed in the same paragraphs, and confidence scores blur the line instead of drawing it.

**Root cause:** the review surface does not separate what code can verify from what a person must judge, and does not show the evidence for either.

**What this concept does about it:** evidence chips on every sentence, deterministic checks with named rules, probabilistic claims with an evidence-strength label and a required adviser agreement, and an audit trail of each decision.
