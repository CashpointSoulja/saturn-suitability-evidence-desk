# V2 roadmap

Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed. Not affiliated with Saturn.

Ordered by expected impact on time to approved report without raising escape rate.

1. **Vulnerability: reviewed classifier behind the lexicon.** Keep the lexicon for recall on known phrasing; add a classifier for phrasing like G15 (“I tire quickly and lose my thread”). Every suggestion still needs adviser confirmation. Ship only after it beats the lexicon on a held-out labelled set.
2. **Firm rule packs.** Firm-specific review windows (the ATR window is already configurable), house charges, preferred wording for disadvantages. Owned by each firm’s compliance lead, versioned, audited.
3. **Sentence-level provenance from drafting.** Take evidence ids from the drafting step directly instead of fixtures, so every live draft arrives with chips.
4. **Compliance sampling view.** Pick files by risk (vulnerable clients, overrides, transfers), show decision history, export for file review.
5. **Ongoing advice review support.** Annual review packs that show what service was delivered and the evidence for it, in light of the FCA’s ongoing advice review (S11).
6. **Edit-distance feedback loop.** Aggregate what advisers change most and feed it back to drafting.
7. **Accessibility pass.** Full keyboard flow through claims and evidence, screen-reader labels for check states.
