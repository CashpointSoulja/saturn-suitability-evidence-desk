# Jobs to be done

Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed. Not affiliated with Saturn. Personas are illustrative, not research participants.

## Adviser
**When** a drafted report for a client I met last week lands in my queue, **I want to** see straight away which statements are checked and which need me, **so I can** sign something I would defend to the client and to a file reviewer without rewriting it.
- Hires the desk for: blockers listed before sign-off; one-click jump from a sentence to what the client said.
- Fires it if: it adds clicks to a clean report, or flags so much that everything looks red.

## Paraplanner
**When** a report comes back with problems, **I want to** know exactly which sentence fails which rule and why, **so I can** fix it once and send it back without another round.
- Hires: the failed-rule reason (“1.5% of £30,000 = £450, but the sentence states £540”); send-back reasons in the audit trail.
- Fires: vague flags that need a conversation to interpret.

## Compliance officer
**When** I sample files for review, **I want to** see what evidence each statement rested on and who decided each judgement call, **so I can** show the firm’s process is consistent, including for vulnerable clients.
- Hires: JSON/CSV audit export with rule ids and evidence ids; vulnerability decisions with reasons.
- Fires: checks that claim to be compliance sign-off.

## Firm owner
**When** I decide whether to roll a tool out to every adviser, **I want to** know it lowers cost to serve without raising complaint or remediation risk, **so I can** take on more clients with the same team.
- Hires: time to approved report trending down with escape rate flat at zero.
- Fires: any escape that reaches a client, or adoption that depends on one enthusiast.
